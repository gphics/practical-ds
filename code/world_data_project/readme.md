# Introduction
Global Country Information Dataset 2023
This comprehensive dataset provides a wealth of information about all countries worldwide, covering a wide range of indicators and attributes. It encompasses demographic statistics, economic indicators, environmental factors, healthcare metrics, education statistics, and much more. With every country represented, this dataset offers a complete global perspective on various aspects of nations, enabling in-depth analyses and cross-country comparisons.


### Exploratory Data Analysis

1. Economic Correlation Analysis: Investigate the relationship between GDP and socio-economic factors like life expectancy, literacy rates, and unemployment.

2. Healthcare & Well-being: Analyze the impact of healthcare metrics (e.g., infant mortality, physicians per thousand) on overall life expectancy across different regions.

3. Environmental Impact Studies: Examine the link between a country's agricultural land percentage, forested area, and its CO2 emissions to understand environmental footprints.

4. Labor & Urbanization Trends: Study the dynamics between urban population percentage, labor force participation, and minimum wage levels.


### Modelling
1. Economic Forecasting (Regression): Use indicators like Tax Revenue (%), Labor Force Participation (%), and Population to predict a country’s GDP.
Models: Linear Regression, Support Vector Regression (SVR), or Random Forest Regressor.
2. Public Health Outcomes (Regression/Classification): Predict Life Expectancy or Infant Mortality based on Physicians per Thousand, Out of Pocket Health Expenditure (%), and Maternal Mortality Ratio.
Models: Multivariate Linear Regression or Gradient Boosting (XGBoost).
3. Sustainability & Emissions (Regression): Forecast CO2-Emissions using Agricultural Land (%), Forested Area (%), and Gasoline Price.
Models: Random Forest or Decision Trees.
4. Regional Classification (Classification): Categorize countries into geographic regions or income groups using their unique profile of features like Birth Rate, Minimum Wage, and Urban_population.
Models: K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN), Random Forest Classifier, or Support Vector Machines (SVM).

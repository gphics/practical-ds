Result of operational efficiency EDA

Task:
Operational Efficiency: How many Hospitals or Doctors have the longest average admission durations, and is there a correlation with the Medical Condition being treated?

Answer:
There are 39876 hospitals and 1186 of them have the highest admission duration of 30 days.
There are  40341 doctors and 1131 of them have the highest admission duration of 30 days.
Using kruskal-wallis test because admission duration is not normally distributed, a pvalue of 0.32 was gotten hence there is no correlation between admission duration and medical condition.
